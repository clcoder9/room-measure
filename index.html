How to Use SpatialMeasure AR

          
        

        

          

            

              1
              

                Scan Floor Surface
                
Aim camera at the floor and sweep slowly to detect the horizontal surface plane.

              

            

            

              2
              

                Pin Corners (90° Snap)
                
Tap screen or Pin Corner to place corner anchors with automatic 90° ortho snapping.

              

            

            

              3
              

                Close Floor Area
                
Snap back to corner #1 or tap Close Floor to compute Shoelace floor area & perimeter.

              

            

            

              4
              

                Ceiling & 3D Volume
                
Aim at ceiling or top of wall and pin height to extrude room volume & wall surface area.
